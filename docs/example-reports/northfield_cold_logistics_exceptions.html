How the health score works

Each active exception is weighted by its risk level (critical 10, high 6, medium 3, low 1; unrated counts as high). An exception counts in full if its worst finding is low or there is none, half if its worst finding is medium, and not at all if it has a high or critical finding. The score is the governed share of the total weight: 21.5 of 26. Bands: 85+ Healthy, 65+ Needs attention, 40+ Weak, 0+ Out of control.
